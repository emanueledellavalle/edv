## Motivation

> _«__Many_ _organizations_ _focus on the_ _need_ _for data_ _scienatists__._ _But_ _another_ _equally_ **_vital_ _role_** _is_ _that_ _of the_ **_business_** **_translator_** **_who_** _can serve_ _as_ _the_ **_link_** **_analytical_** **_talent_**_and_ _practical_ _applications_**_to business_** **_questions_**_.»_

## Target of the course

-   No technicalities or programming skills
-   Aimed at informed decision making on data driven projects
-   Strategic planning, project management,
-   product development, team management,
-   buy or make decisions

## Agenda & Material

The course covers two days with a mix of inspirational, theoretical, and workgroup activities. The material refers to the Amsterdam edition where prof. [Marco Brambilla](https://marco-brambilla.com/) and [Emanuele Della Valle](http://emanueledellavalle.org/) covered the theoretical \[T\] parts, [Herminio Vazquez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/herminio-vazquez-701bb0/) and [Riccardo Vincelli](https://www.linkedin.com/in/riccardo-vincenzo-vincelli-24b79160) gave the inspirational keynotes \[I\] and participants performed the workgroup activities \[W\].
